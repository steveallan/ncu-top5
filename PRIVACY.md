# NCU Top 5: Privacy Policy

_Last updated 14 October 2026_

NCU Top 5 is a tool used by the Northern Cricket Union (NCU) to post weekly "Top 5" round
highlights (batting, bowling, partnerships and fielding) to the NCU's own Facebook Page.

## What it uses

- **Match figures from PlayHQ.** The tool reads the public scorecards of NCU matches published on
  PlayHQ / play.cricket.com.au: players' names as shown on those scorecards, their club, and their
  runs, wickets and catches. Players whose PlayHQ profile is set to private are shown only as
  "Private player".
- **The NCU Facebook Page.** The tool uses Facebook's Pages API only to publish posts to the NCU
  Page, on behalf of an NCU administrator who approves each post before it goes up.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect, read or store any information about people who use Facebook, follow the
  NCU Page, or interact with its posts.
- It does not use Facebook Login for anyone other than the NCU administrator who set it up.
- It does not sell or share any data, or use it for advertising.

## Keeping and removing information

The cards and captions made for each round are kept by the NCU as a record of what was posted.
If you would like your name removed from a card or post, or would not like to be included in
future, email **admin.ncu@gmail.com**. We will remove it from the Facebook post and leave you out
of future cards.
